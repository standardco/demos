Who does what in the truancy process, how work passes between people and systems, and roughly how long each manual step takes. Weekly hours are district-wide estimates.

Based on what we know, KCK staff likely spend about 45 hours a week (181 a month, roughly 1.1 full-time people) on truancy paperwork. We think Gemini agents could cut that by about half, without buying new software.

How to read the times

Per-unit times (for example "3–5 min / letter") are our estimates. Weekly hours apply them to a best-guess volume of ~6,000 first letters a year, worked back from ~1,700 referrals, over 36 school weeks.

Where AI stops

Gemini works in Gmail, Docs, Sheets and Forms but can't act inside Infinite Campus. Steps that write to IC one student at a time stay manual unless KCK IT confirms a batch import.

Not shown

Coding absences as excused or unexcused and returning parent calls. That's likely a large share of secretary time, but it sits outside the truancy steps.
