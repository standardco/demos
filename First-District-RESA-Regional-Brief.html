Regional data brief

First District RESA — Georgia's published data, 2024–25

All 19 member districts. Every figure on this page is a value published by the State of Georgia, reproduced as-is. Nothing here is calculated, blended, averaged, or ranked by Standard Education.

What is and isn't here. Georgia publishes a statewide figure for chronic absenteeism, graduation rate, and enrollment characteristics, so those are shown against the state. Georgia does not publish a statewide row for Milestones assessment results or for discipline, so those are shown on their own absolute scale with no state comparison — rather than against a state average we would have had to compute ourselves. There is also no published First District RESA figure for anything, so this page shows no regional total.

What Georgia's numbers show

    Achievement levels as Georgia reports them

    The four published achievement-level percentages for each district, stacked. The vertical rule marks Georgia's proficiency standard — segments to its right are students at Proficient or Distinguished. Districts are ordered by the published Beginning Learner percentage.

    Chronic absenteeism, as published by student group

    Each district's published rate for students with and without disabilities. Dashed lines are Georgia's published statewide rates for the same two groups.

    Graduation rate against Georgia's published rate

    Published 4-year cohort rate per district. The line is Georgia's published statewide rate of %.

    Suspension, as published

    Share of students given in-school and out-of-school suspension at least once. Georgia publishes no statewide row in this file, so there is no state line to draw.

    Published PBIS implementation ratings

    Georgia publishes a PBIS implementation rating for individual schools. Select a district to see its schools and their published ratings. A school with no rating is not necessarily a school without PBIS — the rating reflects a submitted state fidelity review.

    Sources. All figures 2024–25, published by Georgia's Governor's Office of Student Achievement / Governor's Office of Education and Workforce Strategy (downloadable data): Georgia Milestones EOG and EOC assessment results; student attendance including chronic absenteeism; 4-year cohort graduation rate; enrollment by subgroup; and the K-12 Student Discipline dashboard files. District boundaries: U.S. Census Bureau TIGER/Line 2023 unified school districts. Member district list: First District RESA. "n/a" means Georgia suppressed or did not publish that cell — most often because the student count was too small to report.
